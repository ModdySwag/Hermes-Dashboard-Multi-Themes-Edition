# Asset Attribution

## Wallpapers

All wallpaper images shipped in this repository are **original works created
by Moddy** (http://www.moddys.net):

- `bundle/lcars-bg.jpg` - the embedded "Enterprise Bridge" photo used by the
  default (Enterprise Bridge) theme.
- `bundle/lcars-bg/*.jpg` - the 12 background images used by the other LCARS
  themes (Nebula Steel, Deep Space Blue, Polar White, Subspace Indigo, Ice
  Cavern, Void Navy, Plasma Cyan, Laser Grid, Signal Spectrum, Glacier Mist,
  Warp Crimson, Ion Teal).
